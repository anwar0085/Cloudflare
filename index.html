Step-By-Step Architecture

How It Works
(Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?)

From your local folder to global edge servers in under 60 seconds — zero terminal commands or complicated server setup required.

STEP 01
📁

Prepare Your Folder

Place your HTML, CSS, images or this exact template inside a single folder on your computer (e.g. my-website/).

STEP 02
☁️

Drop into Cloudflare

Go to Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > Create > Upload static files. Type your project name and drag-drop the folder.

STEP 03
🌍

Deploy in 3 Seconds

Cloudflare compiles and distributes your assets across 300+ global edge locations worldwide on Region: Earth.

STEP 04
🔒

Connect Custom Domain

Click "Custom domains" > Add your domain (e.g. ninetech.cloud). Automatic 1-click CNAME DNS & Free SSL generated!

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about lifetime free hosting on Cloudflare.

Kya Cloudflare Pages sach me 100% free hai ya koi hidden charges hain? +
Haan, bilkul 100% free hai. Cloudflare Pages ka free tier unlimited bandwidth aur 500 builds/month allow karta hai. Static websites ke liye aapko kabhi bhi koi card details ya hosting fee nahi deni hoti.
Kya mai apna custom domain (.com, .in, .cloud) connect kar sakta hoon? +
Haan! Aap GoDaddy, Namecheap ya kisi bhi registrar se liya hua domain Cloudflare Pages me 1-click CNAME record add karke bind kar sakte hain. Automatic Free SSL bhi activate ho jata hai.
Isme WordPress chalega kya? +
Nahi, Cloudflare Pages Static & Jamstack websites ke liye hai (HTML, CSS, JS, React, Vue, Next.js, Canva templates, Astro, Tailwind). Static sites WordPress se 10x zyada fast hoti hain aur unhe hack karna impossible hota hai kyunki koi vulnerable SQL database nahi hota!
Website par contact form ya leads kaise capture karein? +
Aap 100% free tools use kar sakte hain jaise Formspree, Web3Forms ya direct WhatsApp Click-to-Chat link laga sakte hain. Har submit par lead seedhe aapke WhatsApp ya email par aayegi.
