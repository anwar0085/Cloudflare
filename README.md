## 🚀 Quickstart: Deploy to Cloudflare in 60 Seconds

### Method 1: Direct Drag & Drop (Zero Code)
1. Download this repo as a ZIP (click the green **Code** button > **Download ZIP**).
2. Go to [dash.cloudflare.com](https://dash.cloudflare.com) and log in.
3. In the left sidebar, click **Workers & Pages** > **Create an application** > **Pages** > **Upload static files**.
4. Type your project name (e.g. `my-awesome-site`).
5. Drag and drop the folder containing `index.html` into the upload box.
6. Click **Deploy site** — your website is live in 3 seconds at `yourproject.pages.dev`!

### Method 2: Connect Custom Domain (yourdomain.com)
1. In your Cloudflare Pages project, click the **Custom domains** tab.
2. Click **Set up a custom domain** and enter your domain (e.g. `ninetech.cloud` or `site.yourdomain.com`).
3. Cloudflare automatically configures the CNAME DNS record and issues an enterprise green SSL padlock 🔒 for free.
